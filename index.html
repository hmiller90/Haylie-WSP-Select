Who We Accept

We consider your school, GPA, concentration, and the qualification call itself — in totality, with no cutoffs. And we'll be honest about what we see: not everyone has a shot at every seat, and we'll tell you what we can and can't accomplish before you commit. What we promise is the part that's ours: you'll go further with us than you would without us.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is WSP Select new? +
    WSP Select launched in [YEAR]. Wall Street Prep didn't — we've trained analysts at bulge bracket banks, elite boutiques, and top universities since 2003. WSP Select is that same training and network, for the first time, built around one dedicated coach.
    What if I don't know what area of finance I want yet? +
    That's what discovery is for. Your coach maps your strengths and interests against the actual roles out there — IB, PE, hedge funds, equity research, asset management — before you commit to a direction.
    How is this different from Wall Street Prep's other courses? +
    [Placeholder — needs a real answer distinguishing WSP Select's 1:1 coaching and platform from WSP's self-study courses.]
    Do you guarantee a job? +
    No. Almost anyone who wants a job in finance can get one eventually — that's not the hard part. The dream offer — the bank, the group, the seat — is. That's what your coach works on with you directly.
    What's the time commitment? +
    About five hours a week — sessions, outreach, mock prep, and the coursework your coach prescribes. It flexes around exams and internships, but the work is real. That's the "willingness to be pushed."
    How does the Wall Street Trek work? +
    Every October, in New York, over two days — included in tuition. We organize the employer visits; you book your own meetings around them with your coach's help. Travel and lodging are on you — we arrange a discounted room block. Open to enrolled students 18 and over at the time of the trip.
    Who will be coaching me? +
    Introduced to your coach at Step 3 of the application process, matched to your target firms and role. See "Your Coach" above.
